# Milo
## Register
product
## Purpose
A downloadable, local desktop companion: a cute thin outlined stickman with hair, sunglasses and baggy trousers. Live articulated motion and 30 exportable sprite animations. Inspired by the movement vocabulary of Fancy Pants 2.
## Direction
Playful, small, unobtrusive. The pet is the main interface. A compact studio provides animation preview and physics controls. User requested autonomous implementation; routine design decisions are inferred.
## Accessibility
Keyboard actions, visible focus, pause and reduced-motion mode. Always available tray quit. No startup persistence by default.
